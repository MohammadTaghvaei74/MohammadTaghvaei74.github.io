# Options liquidity — supersedes seven-day rule
User-approved: 2026-10-17.
Each leg must independently have current Tehran trading-day cumulative trade value strictly greater than 15,000,000,000 toman. No historical average, minimum history length, or completed-day condition. Missing, nonfinite, negative, or other-day value fails closed. Prices still require the existing freshness and other strategy filters. Legacy history fields remain null/zero for compatibility, not eligibility. This applies to the published feed consumed by the website/WebView; no automatic trade orders are enabled.
